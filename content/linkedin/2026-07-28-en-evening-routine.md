A distributor in Jalandhar showed me his evening routine.

Forty supplier bills on the table. One operator. Three hours of typing — item, quantity, rate, tax, line by line. Then the stock register. Then, somewhere near midnight, the realisation that a rate was entered wrong two weeks ago, and every margin since has been quietly wrong.

That's the job Dhela does now.

Photograph the pile with a phone. AI reads every line, updates stock, and recalculates the true weighted-average cost of what you're holding — so your margin is the real one, not the one on the price list. A 45-minute bill becomes about two.

From there: GST invoices, e-way bills as NIC-ready JSON, receivables ageing, and GSTR-1 working papers your accountant can check before filing.

It runs in English, हिंदी and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, because your staff should work in the language they think in.

Free plan, no card. Keep Tally alongside if you want — most people do.

The complaint I hear most about Marg is never the sticker price. It is the renewal.

Marg's licence is one-time. The Annual Renewal Contract is not — and it runs at roughly 25% to 35% of the licence cost, every year.

Take Silver at ₹12,600. A quarter to a third of that is ₹3,150 to ₹4,410 a year, plus GST. Over five years the renewals cost as much as the software did, or more. And unlike the licence, that number is quoted to you fresh each time by someone whose margin depends on it.

You also cannot check it against anything. On Capterra, Marg's price reads "Contact vendor for pricing". On Software Advice, "pricing available upon request". Third-party sites quote the Basic edition anywhere between ₹7,200 and ₹10,300.

On Trustpilot, Marg sits at 2.6 out of 5 across 110 reviews. One of them puts it plainly: "overpriced renewal cost… they make money in renewal time." Another says features already included get charged for again after the annual renewal.

None of that makes it bad software. It bills offline, which in a godown with patchy internet decides whether you can invoice at all. Its pharma batch and expiry handling has twenty years of edge cases in it. If your dealer turns up on a Saturday when billing is down, that is worth paying for. Stay.

But if you are shifting, be clear about what you are shifting for. Most distributors asking this question are not overpaying for software — they are losing three hours an evening to typing supplier bills, and every ERP on the shortlist hands you that same typing.

Our price, since the whole complaint is that nobody can find Marg's: ₹0, ₹3,999 a year, or ₹7,999 a year. GST included. No dealer quote, no AMC, no separate renewal.
